Privacy Policy

vault-tasks-sync · last updated 30 September 2026

vault-tasks-sync is a personal tool built and used by Austin Crozier. It is not offered to the public and has a single user, its owner.

What it accesses

With the owner's consent it reads and writes the owner's own Google Tasks, using the Google Tasks API, to keep them in step with a private notes repository.

What it stores

Task titles, due dates and completion state are held on the owner's own server for the sync to work. Nothing is stored anywhere else.

What it shares

Nothing. No data is sold, shared with third parties, used for advertising, or used to train any model.

Deleting data

The owner can revoke access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions and delete the stored sync data from their own server.
